# Student Performance Analysis Dashboard

Power BI dashboard analyzing student performance across math, reading, and writing scores — broken down by gender, ethnicity, parental education, and test preparation status.

## 📌 Overview

This dashboard analyzes performance data for 100 students, providing insights into how different demographic and preparation factors relate to academic scores.

- **Total Students:** 100
- **Average Math Score:** 62.51
- **Average Reading Score:** 60.49
- **Average Writing Score:** 60.53
- **Average Overall Score:** 61.18

## 📊 Key Insights

- **Test preparation matters most:** Students who completed test preparation scored noticeably higher (53.53%) than those who didn't (46.47%) — the strongest driver of performance in the data.
- **Gender:** Minimal difference between male (49.92%) and female (50.08%) students.
- **Ethnicity:** Scores range narrowly between groups (60–63), showing no strong impact from ethnicity alone.
- **Parental Education:** Top 5 education levels shown; "Some College" and "Bachelor's Degree" backgrounds correlate with slightly higher scores, though the relationship isn't strictly linear.
- **Top performers:** Students S069, S011, S070, S044, and S098 had the highest combined scores across all three subjects.
